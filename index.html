What we're trying to do

Prices for the same coin sometimes differ between exchanges on Base. If you could buy in the cheaper pool and sell in the pricier one, the difference is profit.

This bot looks for those gaps every 30 seconds. For each one, it works out whether the round trip would still make money after trading fees, price slippage and gas. It then simulates the full trade on-chain for free, using a Balancer flash loan so no capital is needed.

  • Paper only. No money is spent and no trades are sent. Results are only logged here.
  • Winning trade = a simulated profit above $0.05 after all costs.
  • Goal: $10 per day in paper profit. Live results would be lower, because faster bots win some of these trades first.
